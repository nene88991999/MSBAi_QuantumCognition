# Discussion 1
Where Does the Classical Model Break Down

M1A1  |  Individual  |  10 points  |  End of Week 1

Choose a real business decision in which asking a question could change the judgment being measured. Explain the problem in terms a manager could use to decide what to test next.

## Prepare Your Post

A detection explanation treats a question as revealing a judgment already formed. A construction explanation allows the act of answering to help form the judgment. In this discussion, the classical benchmark means a fixed set of answers or response rates that carries across the procedures you compare. More general classical models can allow the procedure to change responses.

1. Identify the company, the decision, and the people whose judgment matters. Link at least one public source supporting the business setting. Separate the source’s facts from your own proposed explanation.

2. Describe exactly what would be asked or presented. Explain what a detection explanation predicts and why a construction explanation might predict something different.

3. Choose one pattern from Module 1: an order effect, a direct response outside the range of assessed response rates, or a failure of one fixed joint description across contexts. Explain the chosen pattern in ordinary language. Describe the observations needed to test it; do not claim a violation from an anecdote.

4. Name one plausible alternative explanation, such as learning new information or differences between respondent groups. Propose a comparison that would help distinguish the explanations, and say what you would conclude from a small, precise result.

## Submission

Post 300–400 words in the Canvas discussion and write one substantive peer response of about 100–150 words. Sources and a brief AI disclosure may sit outside the word count. Use the exact dates shown in Canvas; allow time to respond to a classmate.

## Peer Response and Assessment

Choose a peer whose setting differs from yours. Identify one assumption in the proposed comparison, explain why it matters, and suggest one concrete improvement. End with a question that would help the author refine the design.

## Use of AI

AI Assessment Scale level 2. AI may help draft the post, but you must check the claims, revise the reasoning in your own words, and take responsibility for the final text. Add a brief note identifying the tool and what you changed, or state that you did not use AI. Apply the same rule to the peer response.
