Assessment IDs and filenames are retained so existing links continue to work. The proposal ID begins with M2 and the final-submission IDs with M4; their due weeks are **3 and 8**, respectively. Canvas supplies exact dates and reply deadlines.

Use the [Project Templates and Collection Guide](M1_4_Project_Templates_and_Collection_Guide.md), [Lumen handout](M1_4_Lumen_Collection_Guide.md), and [GitHub project workflow](M1_8_GitHub_Student_Workflow.md). The [Python folder](../06_Python/README.md) contains the individual practice notebook, collector, and team audit starter. Save the Week 3 proposal, pilot and freeze the plan in Week 4, collect in Weeks 5–6, and analyze and peer-review in Week 7.

The team presents for ten minutes, and each member completes a five-minute oral defense without live AI assistance or teammate prompting. Review the separate team and individual rubrics in M4A1. Each student writes their own reflection under its stated AI policy. The agency material informs the existing interpretation and recommendation criteria and adds no graded assignment or collection arm.

The [live-session guides](../03_Live_Sessions/README.md) specify preparation and the work to take away. Keep graded submissions, credentials, and raw interviews in approved private locations. The collector makes live model calls only when explicitly enabled; practice and analysis notebooks use supplied or saved records.
