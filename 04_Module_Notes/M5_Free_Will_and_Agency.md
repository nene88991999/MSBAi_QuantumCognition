# Free Will and Agency

Read the [philosophy primer](M1M5_Philosophy_Primer.docx) before the Module 5 live sessions. It defines ontology, epistemology, agency, and conceptual engineering through a notification-setting example. It then introduces the three proposed choice axioms, a worked quantum construction, and a classical process with memory that reproduces the displayed probabilities.

The axioms come from Yang’s current free-will paper. A1 concerns openness and the absence of a distribution of rankings held through each sequence. A2 excludes distributions of fixed answer sheets. A3 concerns standalone probabilities and one value ranking. A2 with openness implies A1; A3 is separate. Their philosophical interpretation needs evidence about the person’s reasons and control.

Use the [transcripts](../07_Lecture_Transcripts/README.md) to revisit the explanations and the [live-session guides](../03_Live_Sessions/README.md) for application. The older book chapter uses different axioms; its numbering is not interchangeable with these agency requirements.
