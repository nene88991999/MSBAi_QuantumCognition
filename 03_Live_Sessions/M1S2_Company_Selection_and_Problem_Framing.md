### Discussion and Work

Spotify introduced playlist exclusion in February 2023. Use the displayed nursery-rhyme example to distinguish an observed play from the listener’s preference for future recommendations. ## Slide 6: Volume for a Complete Audit

What volume can you collect and document?

Six conditions per persona. Counts exclude retries and pilot work.

| Number of personas | One repeat | Three repeats |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 120 | 360 |
| 40 | 240 | 720 |
| 60 | 360 | 1080 |
| 80 | 480 | 1440 |
| 100 | 600 | 1800 |

**Units:** Completed condition records. Values for percentage-formatted rate charts are stored as proportions (0.64 means 64%).

### Discussion and Work

A record here is one completed condition for one persona and repetition. Required records equal personas × six conditions × repetitions. This is accounting, not a precision guarantee, and records need not equal API calls or token costs. Check access, time, approved spending, and the number of calls within each conversation. A pilot tests the workflow. Use the collection guide and record the scope feedback before project collection.
