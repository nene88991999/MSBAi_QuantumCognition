## Slide 2: The Practice Result



Did the calculation recover the simulator’s pattern?

How did the smaller sample change the intervals?

Which warning needs attention?



### Discussion and Work

Students compare estimates with known population values in their own run. A small sample can leave a decision unresolved even when the code is correct. Do not alter seeds until a preferred finding appears.



## Slide 3: The Collection Records



Check one complete persona block.

Confirm six conditions and actual retained answers.

Reconcile interview counts with the collection log.



### Discussion and Work

Use validate_records and the guide. Inspect raw messages as well as the tidy record. Repeated IDs, missing conditions, and unasked questions coded as zero require resolution before analysis.



## Slide 4: The Audit Calculation



Execute from a fresh notebook state.

Trace one answer-pair table to the input records.

Save estimates, intervals, and diagnostic notes.



### Discussion and Work

Allow independent computation and instructor troubleshooting. The default starter uses simulation; students must explicitly switch to model_data for their actual project and supply metadata. Work with a workflow pilot if approved collection remains pending.



## Slide 5: An Uncertain Result



Check coding, missingness, and subgroup size.

Examine the frozen thresholds.

Distinguish imprecision from a software error.



### Discussion and Work

Sparse assessed subgroups can make bracket intervals unavailable. A constant outcome can give a degenerate interval. Additional bootstrap draws do not substitute for relevant data. Preserve honest unresolved results.



## Slide 6: Peer Critique of the Evidence



Does each estimate have a clear denominator?

Does the interval preserve the sampling structure?

Does the interpretation stay within Tier 2?



### Discussion and Work

Exchange a summary table without sharing private or unnecessary raw records. Use the Part 2 rubric to identify one claim to narrow or one check to strengthen.



## Slide 7: The Result Paragraph



State the tested model, procedure, and outcome.

Report magnitude and uncertainty together.

Connect the finding to a proposed action.



### Discussion and Work

Draft the report using actual results or explicitly mark a workflow draft if collection is incomplete. Explain the strongest alternative near the interpretation it limits.



## Slide 8: Remaining Work



Save the notebook, records, and revised evidence table.

Log any departure from the proposal.

Assign your next analysis or collection step.



### Discussion and Work

The report is individual. Preserve the original design and explain revisions. Consult Canvas and M4A1 for the submission and presentation requirements.
