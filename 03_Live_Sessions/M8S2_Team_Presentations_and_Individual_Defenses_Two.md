# Team Presentations and Individual Defenses Two

M8S2 · 90 minutes

Watch the videos before the meeting. Bring the final team deck and reviewed report. Each student prepares to explain the whole audit independently. Check the separately posted presentation and defense slots.

**Work to take away:** Completed scheduled team presentations and individual defenses, feedback for the final report, and individual reflection notes.

## 0–5 Minutes · The Presentation and Defense Are Distinct

| Component | Who responds | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client presentation | Team | 10 minutes |
| Oral defense | Individual | 5 minutes per student |

Follow the posted slots; every student has a defense.

Team report and deck are shared work. The oral defense is separately individual, with no live AI assistance or teammate prompting. Keep transitions brief. Timings are the existing format, not new grade weights.

## 5–45 Minutes · Scheduled Presentation and Defense Block

State the decision and the comparison.

Show the strongest evidence and a credible rival.

Explain the recommendation and its review condition.

Use this slide between presentations. Replace it with each team’s own deck during the presentation. The 40-minute block follows the posted timetable, which can combine team presentations and individual defenses. Do not use generic slides as a substitute for the student presentation.

## 45–85 Minutes · Continue the Posted Slots

Keep each response within its assigned time.

Individual speakers explain their own reasoning.

Observers record a question and a useful comparison.

Continue the scheduled presentation or defense slots. The instructor selects questions about assumptions, uncertainty, rival mechanisms, managerial action, agency, welfare, and documented revisions. Observers give feedback when the timetable permits. Do not reveal assessment answers in advance.

## 85–90 Minutes · Record Feedback and Complete the Week

Save the feedback that changes a claim or action.

Complete the team report, deck, and required logs.

Submit your own reflection using the posted deadline.

The two regular meetings provide 160 minutes of presentation and defense time, after opening and closing activities. For 22 students, defenses take 110 minutes. Seven team talks add 70 minutes; eight add 80. Reserve a 30-minute overflow block and post each student’s slot. With seven teams, 20 minutes of overflow is required; with eight, all 30 minutes is required. This reserve must be scheduled; the deck does not claim it has been booked. Exact due dates and slot assignments are in Canvas.
