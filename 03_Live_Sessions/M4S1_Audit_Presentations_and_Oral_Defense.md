# M4S1: Audit Presentations and Oral Defense



**Preparation:** Prepare the individual 6–10 slide deck and rehearse a ten-minute presentation. Review M4A1 and the no-AI oral-defense rule.



**Session output:** The individual presentation and oral defense, plus recorded feedback for the final report.



This 90-minute meeting assumes the videos have been watched. The activities support the assessments listed below; follow each assessment brief for individual submission requirements.

## Slide 2: Presentation Block



Ten-minute individual presentation.

Five minutes of questions.

Up to six presenters in a 90-minute block.



### Discussion and Work

Display before presentations. The 90 minutes belong to six 15-minute slots. Briefing instructions should be read before the meeting. If the cohort exceeds six, the instructor schedules additional blocks. The following slides are reference prompts and do not add time.



## Slide 3: The Client Story



Decision and comparison.

Design, evidence, uncertainty, and strongest alternative.

Recommendation, owner, and review condition.



### Discussion and Work

Reference checklist for the student’s own 6–10 slides. Show the evidence that affects the decision and keep complete counts and code available for questions.



## Slide 4: Questions About the Evidence



Which result would change your recommendation?

Which assumption carries your broadest claim?

What remains unresolved?



### Discussion and Work

These are public preparation questions. The oral defense is AIAS 0. Students explain their own analysis without AI assistance during the defense.



## Slide 5: Peer Feedback



Identify one well-supported claim.

Identify one missing link between evidence and action.

Ask one specific question.



### Discussion and Work

Peer feedback supports revision and does not replace the instructor’s grading. Keep comments focused on the stated decision, design, evidence, and interpretation.



## Slide 6: After the Presentation



Record the feedback that affects your recommendation.

Revise the report and document the change.

Retain the evidence supporting your final judgment.



### Discussion and Work

Use the revision log in the collection guide. The final written submission remains governed by M4A1 and the Canvas deadline.
