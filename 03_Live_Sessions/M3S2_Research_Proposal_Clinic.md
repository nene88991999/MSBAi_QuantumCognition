# Research Proposal Clinic

M3S2 · 90 minutes

Watch the videos before the meeting. Watch Module 3 and complete the individual audit practice on supplied data. Bring the team proposal draft, six-condition battery, pilot plan, and contribution record.

**Work to take away:** A team proposal ready for the end of Week 3, with unresolved pilot questions assigned for Week 4.

## 0–10 Minutes · The Proposal and the Collection Plan

| Week | Decision or output |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | Submit the research proposal |
| 4 | Pilot and freeze the collection plan |
| 5–6 | Collect under the frozen plan |

What still needs a decision before submission?

The proposal is shared team work. Audit practice remains individual and ungraded. A pilot checks implementation; it is not the main collection. Do not present tentative grading weights as confirmed.

## 10–25 Minutes · Match the Claim to a Comparison

| Claim | Comparison to specify |
| --- | --- |
| Order changes a judgment | AB versus BA under matched conditions |
| An assessment changes F | Direct F versus assessed A then F |
| A model predicts agreement | QQ restriction with uncertainty |

Name the outcome, denominator, and rival explanation.

Teams mark the exact question and population for each proposed claim. Use the established six-condition collection guide. A QQ result concerns aggregate paired-response probabilities and does not show that every person is internally consistent.

## 25–40 Minutes · A Pilot Record That Can Be Replayed

| Field | Example to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Condition and run ID | Constructed AB, persona 07, repetition 02 |
| Prompt and actual answer | A: yes, then B: no, then F: yes |
| Model and settings | Exact identifier, settings, date, retries |

Can another team reconstruct this response path?

Use a small synthetic pilot or supplied demonstration. Retain actual responses in constructed conversations. Fresh-context arms use separate conversations. Preserve failed and retried calls with identifiers. Verify that a retry is not silently counted as a new respondent.

## 40–60 Minutes · Peer Review the Team Proposal

Exchange the claim and design sections.

Identify one ambiguous choice and its consequence.

Write a concrete revision with a reason.

Review against the current team proposal rubric. Give each student an assigned review role. Check the company decision, sources, six conditions, model-panel scope, thresholds, analysis plan, and division of work. Avoid requesting new data just to make a result more favorable.

## 60–80 Minutes · Resolve the Highest Priority Issues

| Issue | Owner | Evidence of resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question wording | Assign a member | Exact final wording |
| Analysis rule | Assign a member | Rule and reason recorded |
| Implementation risk | Assign a member | Pilot check in Week 4 |

Revise the shared proposal and contribution log.

This is a blank planning structure, not a prescribed division among particular people. Every member should understand the full design. Record what changed and why. Keep unresolved issues visible rather than silently filling them with assumed values.

## 80–90 Minutes · Prepare the Week 3 Submission

One proposal, with all team members identified.

Attach the battery, analysis plan, and attribution records.

Carry implementation checks into the Week 4 pilot.

The proposal is due at the end of Week 3; Canvas supplies the exact date. Main collection opens in Week 5. Finish with each member explaining one design choice and one condition that could change the interpretation.
