# Authority and Consumer Interests

M6S2 · 90 minutes

Watch the videos before the meeting. Watch Module 6 L2 through the conclusion. Bring the team’s evidence table and a specific recommendation under consideration.

**Work to take away:** A recommendation paragraph that identifies the affected user, procedural authority, valuation assumptions, and a testable review condition.

## 0–20 Minutes · Follow the User’s Actual Path

| Path A | Path B |
| --- | --- |
| Reminder is requested | Reminder is required |
| Skip reaches the decision | Skip returns to the reminder |
| Permission can be withdrawn | No effective direct route |

What observation would verify each claimed control?

These are fictional interface paths, not screenshots of a real service. Students may draw the paths in their notes. Examine understanding, effort, accessibility, and the point at which the decision becomes binding. Equal final outcomes leave procedural authority unresolved.

Source basis: Nathan Yang, Agency and the Persistence of Choice, November 2026 draft; Agency Erosion, October 2026 draft; course philosophy primer. Practice data are constructed and are not observed human or model-panel results.

## 20–40 Minutes · A Different Welfare Calculation

| Category | Expected loss |
| --- | --- |
| Direct | 2 |
| After reminder | 3.5 |

Constructed practice example; baseline is always declining.

What is the additional expected loss, and under which values?

Practice values: renewal probabilities are 20% and 35%; declining is independently worth $10 more than renewing. Expected losses are $2 and $3.50; additional loss is $1.50. Hold the offer, values, and procedure costs fixed. If values are unknown, a monetary claim needs further evidence.

Source basis: Nathan Yang, Agency and the Persistence of Choice, November 2026 draft; Agency Erosion, October 2026 draft; course philosophy primer. Practice data are constructed and are not observed human or model-panel results.

## 40–55 Minutes · Preserve Useful Delegation

| Permission | Control to evaluate |
| --- | --- |
| Show an optional reminder | Effective skip |
| Choose a review sequence | Understand and revise the scope |
| Take a binding action | Confirm before acting |

Redesign one path without assuming all reminders are harmful.

Groups specify permission, refusal, and revision. Requested, informed, revocable delegation can support the user’s purpose. A redesign is a proposal; identify what user evidence would establish that it works.

Source basis: Nathan Yang, Agency and the Persistence of Choice, November 2026 draft; Agency Erosion, October 2026 draft; course philosophy primer. Practice data are constructed and are not observed human or model-panel results.

## 55–65 Minutes · Apply the Comparison to the Team Recommendation

Name the person and goal affected.

Separate the response effect from the welfare claim.

State which authority the proposed change preserves.

A synthetic-panel result supports a claim about model behavior under the tested protocol. Identify human or valuation evidence that would be needed before a broader conclusion. Keep the original company decision central.

Source basis: Nathan Yang, Agency and the Persistence of Choice, November 2026 draft; Agency Erosion, October 2026 draft; course philosophy primer. Practice data are constructed and are not observed human or model-panel results.

## 65–80 Minutes · Write a Review Condition

| Recommendation | Evidence that could change it |
| --- | --- |
| Offer a direct route | Refusal fails or users misunderstand |
| Choose a stated sequence | Effect or rival check changes |
| Make a welfare claim | Values or offer no longer match |

Specify a measure, owner, and practical threshold.

Use the team’s justified threshold rather than inventing one after seeing results. Make the review condition specific enough for a manager to act. Connect it to the report’s evidence table and the revision log.

Source basis: Nathan Yang, Agency and the Persistence of Choice, November 2026 draft; Agency Erosion, October 2026 draft; course philosophy primer. Practice data are constructed and are not observed human or model-panel results.

## 80–90 Minutes · Prepare for Analysis and Peer Critique

Save the recommendation and its assumptions.

List missing evidence as an explicit next step.

Bring the report draft and working figures to Week 7.

Collection is closed. Week 7 is for troubleshooting, analysis, peer review, and drafting. Any further data collection requires a documented reason and must not be presented as part of the original frozen design.
