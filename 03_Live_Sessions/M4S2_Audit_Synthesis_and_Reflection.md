## Slide 2: The Scope of the Evidence



What was the unit of observation?

Which procedure changed?

Which conclusion was best supported?



### Discussion and Work

Students answer from their own audit. Use the three questions to establish comparable descriptions before discussing patterns across projects.



## Slide 3: Findings Across Audits



Compare the procedures and measured outcomes first.

Look for shared patterns and important differences.

Retain unresolved and practically small results.



### Discussion and Work

Build a class synthesis using volunteered summaries. Different models, questions, persona grids, and settings limit direct comparisons. A tally of positive results is not a pooled estimate.



## Slide 4: Recommendations Across Settings



Which findings led to a process change?

Which called for additional evidence?

Which supported retaining the current procedure?



### Discussion and Work

Ask students to justify each action using the actual decision rule and costs. Strong analysis can support any of these outcomes. Distinguish a managerial judgment from a unique mechanism claim.



## Slide 5: The Strongest Alternative



What evidence favors the competing explanation?

Which additional comparison would be informative?

How would it affect your recommendation?



### Discussion and Work

Students revisit the rival specified in Part 1 and the fair test they proposed. If the audit did not distinguish explanations, document that directly and propose a concrete next comparison.



## Slide 6: The Revised Recommendation



Name the action and responsible owner.

State the evidence that supports it.

Add a condition for review.



### Discussion and Work

Individual revision time for Project Part 2. Connect the recommendation to the strongest defensible finding. Keep broader human claims tied to the human evidence that would be needed.



## Slide 7: Reflection Notes



What did you initially expect?

What evidence changed or confirmed your reasoning?

Which habit will you carry into another decision?



### Discussion and Work

The reflection is AIAS 1. Students write their own reflection; AI may be used only for permitted brainstorming. Use this period for independent notes from the proposal, results, and revision log. The target is 800 words, with details in M4A2.



## Slide 8: Final Submission Check



Report, executed notebook, records, deck, and logs.

Check that the figures match the reported estimates.

Submit through the Canvas assignment links.
