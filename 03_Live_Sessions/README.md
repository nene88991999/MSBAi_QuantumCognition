The Module 4 presentation block requires ten minutes per team plus five minutes per student for individual oral defenses. Three four-person teams use 90 minutes. The instructor schedules additional blocks when needed and posts team assignments and slots in Canvas. The defense is individual and uses no live AI assistance or teammate prompting.

The proposal and final audit report with its deck are team submissions. Discussions, Module 3 practice, oral defenses, and reflections remain individual. See the [assessment allocation](../02_Assessments/README.md) for both Part 1 and full-course weights. Workshop outputs prepare those existing assessments.

PowerPoint files contain editable text, tables, and data charts. PDFs and Markdown guides provide reading alternatives. The design follows Nathan Yang’s Marketing Analytics course, with parchment backgrounds, dark blue text, orange rules, and large visuals. Published findings, product demonstrations, and imposed teaching data are labeled separately. Source links and image descriptions appear in the guides and slide notes.
