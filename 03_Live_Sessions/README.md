# Live Sessions for the Eight-Week Course

Watch the relevant videos before each 90-minute meeting. The sessions use cases, calculations, peer critique, and workshop time to advance the existing assessments. Modules 5–6 introduce philosophy and agency; Weeks 7–8 use studio and presentation formats.

The proposal is due at the end of Week 3. Freeze the collection plan in Week 4, collect in Weeks 5–6, analyze and peer-review in Week 7, and complete presentations, individual defenses, the report/deck, and reflection in Week 8. Canvas supplies exact dates and confirmed grading rules. This schedule replaces the earlier four-week live-session calendar.

The proposal and report with deck are team work. Discussions, practice, oral defenses, and reflections remain individual. Workshops prepare those existing assessments; the new agency exercises do not add graded submissions or require changes to a frozen collection plan.

Presentations last ten minutes per team; defenses last five minutes per student. For 22 students and seven or eight teams, reserve a 30-minute overflow block alongside the two Week 8 meetings. The regular meetings allocate 160 minutes to assessed slots; seven teams need 180 minutes and eight need 190. The instructor must post every team and individual slot, including the overflow block.

The decks follow the established Marketing Analytics reference style: parchment background, dark blue text, orange rules, large labels, and editable tables/charts. Guides reproduce the data and describe images for accessibility. Speaker notes contain activity instructions, evidence qualifications, and sources. [Practice data and Python](../06_Python/M5_6_Agency_Practice.md) support the agency workshops.
