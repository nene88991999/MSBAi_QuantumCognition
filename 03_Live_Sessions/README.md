The Module 4 presentation session allows six students to present for ten minutes and answer questions for five minutes each. Larger cohorts need additional presentation blocks. The other guides allocate exactly 90 minutes. Workshop outputs are preparation for the existing assessment, rather than additional graded submissions.

PowerPoint files contain editable text, tables, and data charts. PDFs and Markdown guides provide reading alternatives. The design follows Nathan Yang’s Marketing Analytics course: parchment backgrounds, dark blue text, restrained orange rules, and large, focused visuals. Official product examples and editable evidence figures support the workshop questions. Published findings, product demonstrations, and imposed teaching data are labeled separately. Source links and image descriptions appear in the session guides and slide notes. See the [visual source register](source/visual_sources.json).
