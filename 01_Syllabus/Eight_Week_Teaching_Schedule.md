# Eight-Week Teaching Schedule

This schedule implements the course extension and governs the live-session sequence. Canvas supplies exact dates and the confirmed full-course grading allocation. The older Part 1 syllabus and assessment briefs retain the original content and rubrics; their four-week timing and 50% course scaling require the course-wide update before student release.

| Week | Content | Assessment Progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Judgment and models | Discussion 1; identify a company decision |
| 2 | Order effects and uncertainty | Discussion 2; develop the proposal |
| 3 | Human–AI decision processes | Discussion 3; individual practice; team proposal due |
| 4 | Evidence and managerial action | Pilot interpretation; freeze the collection plan |
| 5 | Free will and agency | Collection opens; definition and evidence paragraph |
| 6 | Agency over time and consumer welfare | Collection closes; practice and recommendation |
| 7 | Troubleshooting and peer critique | Analysis, draft report/deck, and revisions |
| 8 | Presentations and individual defenses | Team report/deck and individual reflection due |

The team proposal and report/deck remain shared submissions. Oral defenses and reflections remain individual. New philosophy exercises prepare existing assessments and add no graded deliverable. The instructor must schedule an overflow defense block when required by enrollment and team count; see the live-session guide.
