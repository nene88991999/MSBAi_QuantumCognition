# Eight-Week Teaching Schedule

This schedule and the [syllabus](Part1_Quantum_Cognition_Syllabus.md) govern the full eight-module course. The course retains 2 credits and sixteen 90-minute live meetings. Canvas supplies exact dates and submission links.

| Week | Module | Assessment Progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why Classical Models Break Down | Discussion 1; select and document a company decision. |
| 2 | Contextuality and Order Effects | Discussion 2; develop the proposal and question battery. |
| 3 | Human-AI Decision Teaming | Discussion 3; individual practice; team proposal due at the end of Week 3. |
| 4 | Audit Verdicts and Managerial Action | Pilot, repair, and freeze the collection plan before the main run. |
| 5 | Free Will and Axiomatic Agency | Collection opens under the frozen plan; develop an ungraded definition-and-evidence paragraph. |
| 6 | Agency over Time and Procedural Control | Collection closes; complete supplied agency practice and refine the recommendation. |
| 7 | Analysis and Peer Critique | Analysis, report and deck drafts, peer critique, and recorded revisions. |
| 8 | Presentations and Individual Defense | Team presentations and individual defenses; report, deck, and individual reflection due. |

The proposal and report/deck are team submissions. Discussions, practice, oral defenses, and reflections are individual. The agency exercises prepare existing assessments and add no graded deliverable. All full-course weights are in the [assessment table](../02_Assessments/README.md).

Both Week 8 sessions include presentations and defenses. The instructor posts individual slots and an overflow block when required by enrollment and team count. The regular decks allow 160 assessment minutes; 22 students and seven or eight teams require 180 or 190 minutes.
